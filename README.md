🛒 Customer Purchasing Behaviors Dataset
This repository contains a comprehensive dataset on customer purchasing behaviors, focusing on shopping patterns, spending habits, 
product preferences, and demographic influences. The dataset is valuable for market analysis, personalized recommendations, sales 
forecasting, and customer segmentation.

📌 Features
📊 Dataset Overview
Customer Demographics: Age, gender, income level, location.
Purchase History: Frequency, total spending, product categories.
Shopping Preferences: Online vs. in-store purchases, seasonal trends.
Product Categories: Electronics, fashion, groceries, beauty, home essentials, etc.
Discount & Promotion Impact: How sales and offers influence purchases.

🏆 Key Insights from the Dataset
Top-selling Products & Categories: Identifying customer favorites.
Customer Segmentation: Clustering buyers based on shopping behavior.
Seasonal Trends: Understanding demand fluctuations across the year.
Loyalty Analysis: Recognizing repeat buyers vs. one-time customers.
Price Sensitivity: Examining how customers react to pricing changes.
🤖 Data Analysis & Machine Learning Applications
Recommendation Systems: Suggesting products based on purchase history.
Market Basket Analysis: Identifying products frequently bought together.
Customer Churn Prediction: Detecting customers likely to stop purchasing.
Time-Series Forecasting: Predicting future sales trends.
Sentiment Analysis: Analyzing customer reviews and feedback.

🚀 Technologies Used
Python: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn for data analysis.
Machine Learning: Scikit-Learn, XGBoost, Time-Series models.
Data Visualization: Tableau, Power BI, Plotly.
